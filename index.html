Project PAG-ASA is an independent initiative working toward safe and sustainable transportation solutions for children who face difficult journeys to school in remote communities in the Philippines.

Discover the Project

About Project PAG-ASA

PAG-ASA means "hope" in Filipino. The project was created around a simple belief: where a child lives should not determine whether they can safely reach their education.

Safe Journeys

We want children to be able to travel to school without having to face unnecessary danger on their daily journey.

Equal Opportunities

Education should remain within reach even for children living in remote and geographically isolated communities.

Local Cooperation

Sustainable solutions must be developed together with schools, communities, local authorities and people who understand the realities on the ground.

The Challenge

For some children, going to school means crossing rivers, walking through difficult terrain, travelling long distances or using unsafe transportation.

These journeys are often invisible to people far away from the communities affected by them. Project PAG-ASA is working to document these situations, understand the challenges and identify where sustainable transportation solutions could make a real difference.

Our Vision

A future where children in remote communities can reach their schools safely, reliably and with dignity.


Project PAG-ASA aims to work toward long-term, locally appropriate transportation solutions rather than temporary fixes.

Research & Network Building

Project PAG-ASA is currently in its research and network-building phase. We are identifying communities and school routes where children face significant transportation challenges, documenting existing situations and seeking contact with schools, education offices, local organizations and people on the ground.
